Aetheris Cloud Systems: the AI productivity paradox

Round 1 case from Gartner. Open all four tabs +15 XP each, then play the diagnostic and run the revenue simulator below.

Round 1 Executive Question

"As commercial strategy consultants presenting to the ACS Executive Committee, analyze the situation above and determine why their AI investment has failed to generate revenue and develop a solution using AI to drive revenue."

The Scenario

Six months ago, Aetheris Cloud Systems (ACS), an enterprise supply chain & logistics SaaS company, launched AetherisCopilot, an in-house AI assistant, to its 300 Account Executives to drive commercial growth. Sellers use it for prospecting, meeting prep and CRM admin.

Early results looked great. Then CRO Marcus Vance hit a paradox: activity surged, revenue didn't.

The Buyer: ACS Executive Committee

You present as commercial strategy consultants to the ACS Executive Committee. The person feeling the pain is CRO Marcus Vance: he owns the revenue number and the AI rollout that isn't paying off.

The core tension (Case 1 "Current Operational Reality")

⚖️ Activity vs. Value Misalignment

Leadership judged the AI rollout on adoption and message volume, assuming more output would linearly yield more revenue.

🧠 Seller Misconceptions

AEs feel more productive because admin takes less time, and reinvest the saved time into more automated cold emails (72% goes to low-value admin & generic blasts).

🙈 Frontline Manager Blindspots

Sales managers still audit traditional volume metrics (calls, emails sent) in weekly 1:1s.

What Marcus needs to hear: a diagnosis that separates symptoms from causes, a buildable AI fix, who owns it, how it's tracked, and what it's worth in ARR.

Gartner's playbook: Strip → Stack → Sharpen

From "Reimagining Sales Productivity in the Age of AI" (Scott Collins, Gartner), the support deck in the case folder.

1 · StripEliminate busywork → easier choices. Account agents watch for buying signals and surface high-propensity accounts with reasons.
2 · StackGuidance on demand → higher standards. Package top-performer expertise into a meeting-prep agent (adaptive discovery guide).
3 · SharpenData-driven coaching. Managers use cross-system diagnostics to focus the 1:1 on the highest-impact behavior.
